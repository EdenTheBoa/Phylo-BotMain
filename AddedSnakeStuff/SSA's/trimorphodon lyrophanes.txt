California Lyresnakes *Trimorphodon lyrophanes* are medium sized (61-91cm, up to 115cm) colubrid snakes that range from southern California south through Baja California, MX, for near sea level up to 1800m.  They principally inhabit dry areas, especially rocky slopes within scrubland, arid grassland, woodland, and forest.

Rear-fanged snakes, *T. lyrophanes* produce a mild venom that helps them subdue small prey items.  They are considered harmless to humans but prolonged, chewing bites should be avoided as a precaution.  Primarily nocturnal, they are most commonly observed at night while crossing roads which bisect their habitat.  Their favorite prey is lizards, but rodents, bats, snakes, and small birds are commonly consumed as well.  They are strong climbers and will sometimes forage in trees and bushes.

California Lyresnakes are fairly slender in build.  The head is distinctive at the neck, with protruding eyes and elliptical pupils.  Dorsal coloration is light brown or light grey, patterned with 23-48 (average 34) [darker brown or reddish brown blotches](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/136329688?size=large) that have a lighter center and sometimes are completely disconnected at the middle.  A distinctive, [dark marking on the head](https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/120349514?size=large) might be shaped like a V, a pair of batwings, or a lyre, inspiring this species' common name.  A dark bar also runs in between (or just in front of) the eyes.  The dorsal scales are smooth and arranged in 19-24 rows at midbody.

California Lyresnakes are sometimes confused with other species.  Sonoran Lyresnakes *T. lambda* average fewer dorsal blotches (18-31, average 24).  In most areas they can be differentiated on the basis of range, but their exact distributional limitations are poorly resolved.  Glossy Snakes *Arizona elegans* lacks the distinctive V shaped marking on top of the head, they have at least 25 dorsal scale rows at midbody, and the head is comparatively elongate.  *Pituophis* Gophersnakes lack the distinctive marking on top of the head, have keeled dorsal scales, at least 27 dorsal scale rows at midbody, and are more robust in build.
